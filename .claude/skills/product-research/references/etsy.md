# Etsy rules and research notes

Limits below are last known. Confirm each against Etsy's current seller documentation (help.etsy.com, "Etsy Seller Handbook") before finalising, and record the date checked. Do not apply Amazon or Shopify conventions here.

## Eligibility check comes first (can force a REJECT)
Etsy is a marketplace for items that are handmade, vintage (20+ years), or craft supplies, and it applies its Creativity Standards (made by, designed by, sourced by, handpicked by). Before any other stage, check whether the product can legitimately be sold there:
- Mass-produced goods bought from a general supplier and resold as-is, including typical dropshipped items, are generally not allowed. Read the current Creativity Standards and "Seller Policy" pages and quote the relevant lines.
- If the product only fits through the seller's own design or production, say what the seller would have to do, and whether the user actually does it.
- If it is not eligible, the verdict is REJECT for Etsy (it may still work on Shopify or elsewhere).

## Where research evidence comes from
- Etsy search autosuggest and related searches (type the seed term, record suggestions).
- Top results for target queries: titles, tags if visible, price, review count, shop age, photo style. Etsy shows "Bestseller" and "Popular now" labels and review/sales counts on some listings. Record only what is shown.
- Reviews on competing listings for pain points and praise.
- Etsy shows listing tags to the public on the listing page in some views. Where visible, record them as VERIFIED.
- Etsy Stats (the seller's own) is the only source of impressions, visits and conversion for the user's own listings.

## How Etsy ranks (from Etsy's own published explanations, re-check)
Relevance of listing text to the query, listing quality (including how shoppers engage and convert), recency, shop's customer and market experience (reviews, shipping speed and cost, response to messages). Etsy also considers context (location, language, past behaviour). Price and free shipping matter to shoppers and to conversion. Keyword stuffing and unrelated tags work against the listing.

## Listing fields (verify limits)
- Title: up to 140 characters. Put the clearest descriptive phrase first because the start is what shows in results, especially on mobile. Readable phrases, not a string of keywords.
- Tags: up to 13, up to 20 characters each. Use all 13 with different multi-word phrases that match buyer searches. Do not repeat the same words across every tag without need.
- Category and attributes: choose the most specific category, fill every relevant attribute (colour, occasion, material, size, style). Attributes feed filters and search.
- Materials: list what is true.
- Description: first lines matter for the listing page and search snippets. Cover what it is, sizes, materials, care, production and shipping time, personalisation, and policy. Use real facts only.
- Photos: use the maximum allowed. Thumbnail first, then scale, detail, lifestyle, variations, packaging. Check the current limit and whether video is supported.
- Variations, personalisation, processing time, shipping profile, and price including shipping all affect conversion.
- Seasonal and gift listings: publish early enough to be indexed ahead of the season.

## Keyword approach
Buyer-intent phrases from autosuggest, competitor titles, and review language. Etsy buyers search by occasion, recipient, style and material (for example "gift for ...", "... for nursery"). Check each candidate against the top results: if the top results show a different product type, the intent does not match.

## Policy and risk checks
Trademark and licensed characters, health or safety claims, and AI-generated image disclosure rules (check current policy). Prohibited items list. Shipping time promises must be met.

## Final audit additions
- Eligible under Creativity Standards (quote the rule).
- Title front-loaded and natural, 13 tags used and distinct, attributes complete.
- First photo works as a small thumbnail.
- Processing time and shipping profile realistic.
- No claim in the copy that the seller cannot support.
