# Site surface brief

Scope: Persuade, design-only static home page (site/index.html) and room page (site/room.html) for the live Drapewell store. Visitor: a homeowner or renter refreshing a room; chooses a room from the store menu, then a product. Real catalog (29 active products, CAD prices, photos hotlinked from the store CDN) is used; internal data (SKUs, supplier ids, stock) is never shown. Product links go to the live store. No cart or checkout is simulated.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm room lookbook. Each room is a spread: one large plate and a short, quiet list of what is in the room. Refuses the hero banner plus identical product tiles.
OWN-WORLD: Cool white walls, deep ink slate type, one deep teal accent for prices and the active room. Light grotesque display at large size, tabular numerals, generous space, hairline rules. Photographs sit in tinted frames so weak or missing images still compose. No cream, no serif display, no terracotta.
STORY: The visitor feels the store is calm and considered, sees rooms rather than categories, opens a room spread, and goes to a product on the store.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Quiet masthead (wordmark, menu-driven room links, cart link); a light-weight headline "Rooms, finished." beside one large plate; below, a numbered room index.
FORM: Room lookbook, Impeccable's pick (position 1 of the ordered list); seed key 6d5eb76a (degraded roll).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
