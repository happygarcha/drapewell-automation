# Drapewell editorial style guide

Use this for product text, collection text, blog posts, FAQ answers and anything an AI assistant writes or summarises about the store.

## Who we are
Drapewell is an online store in Surrey, British Columbia, Canada. It sells home textiles and decor, outdoor gear, pet supplies, desk finds and accessories. Orders are made and dispatched by supplier partners and ship with tracking to Canada and the US. Prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD).

## Tone
- Plain, friendly and factual. Short sentences. Say what the item is, its size, what it is made of and what is in the box.
- Write for a shopper who wants to know if it fits and what it costs to get here.
- Be honest about gaps: if the supplier does not state a size, weight, material or care instruction, say so. Never guess.

## Spelling and format
- Canadian spelling: colour, favourite, organiser (in product titles use the form the product page uses), centre.
- Sizes in centimetres with inches in brackets where useful. Prices as CA$ or C$ with two decimals.
- Product titles 45 to 70 characters. SEO title 60 or fewer. Meta description 130 to 155 characters.

## Always include (where it applies)
- Size, material, colours and what is included.
- Shipping sentence: "Ships with tracking. Estimated delivery is 13-25 days to Canada and 10-20 days to the US, counted from the order date. Taxes are extra and shipping is calculated by weight at checkout."
- Returns: "If an item arrives damaged or wrong, we replace it or refund you when you contact us within 30 days of delivery. Change-of-mind returns are not accepted."

## Never write
- Health, skin, medical, pain-relief or performance claims (no "relieves", "treats", "supports your spine", "cooling", "anti-aging", "waterproof" unless the supplier states it for that exact item).
- Brand or trademark names that are not ours, except on a product the owner has approved to keep its brand (for example a Shopify Collective product).
- Ratings, review counts, awards, "as seen in", "best seller", "#1", "top rated" or any social proof that is not real and visible on our own pages.
- Invented numbers: sales figures, demand, competitor prices, "limited stock", countdowns.
- Delivery promises beyond the ranges above. No "fast", "express" or "guaranteed" delivery.
- Supplier copy copied word for word. Rewrite it in our own words and keep only facts we can trace.

## Preferred words
tracked shipping, ships with tracking, sold individually, sold as a set of 2, what is included, size guide, measure before you order, colours may vary slightly between screens, supplier measurement.

## Words and phrases to avoid
miracle, premium quality (unless a fact backs it), luxury, perfect for everyone, guaranteed, risk-free, scientifically proven, doctor recommended, anti-bacterial, hypoallergenic, non-toxic (unless the supplier states it for that item), best, cheapest, viral, trending, TikTok famous.

## Store facts to keep consistent
- Location: Surrey, British Columbia, Canada.
- Ships to: Canada and the United States.
- Currency: CAD.
- Shipping: calculated by weight, shown at checkout before you pay.
- Delivery: 13-25 days to Canada, 10-20 days to the US.
- Photos are the supplier's examples. Blurred areas in photos hide third-party marks and are not a product defect.
