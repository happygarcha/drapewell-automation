# Drapewell dropshipping playbook (written 2026-10-08)

Reading copy for the owner is the published "Drapewell Dropshipping Playbook" page. This file is what Claude follows. CLAUDE.md rules take precedence.

## Operating rules
- Nothing published, created, spent or deleted without the owner's yes. Drafts and checks only until approved.
- Every product passes the publish gate (CLAUDE.md) plus the competitor price check before going live. Failing products stay draft.
- No invented numbers. Unchecked items are written "not checked".
- No other seller's logo, watermark or brand on photos, titles or text. No health, skin or performance claims unless supplier data states them.
- Live theme never edited directly. Channels: Online Store, Google & YouTube, Pinterest, Microsoft Copilot, Meta AI and Muse. Shopify Collective paused (owner, 2026-10-08).

## Money tests
- Profit = 0.971 x (price + shipping band) - 0.30 - landed; landed = (CJ USD + freight USD) x 1.42443. Need >= CA$12.
- Checkout total within about 1.5x typical Canadian competitor total (Amazon.ca / Walmart.ca; owner confirms by hand).
- Target: under 600 g, CA$25-60 item price.

## Pipeline
1 Find (CJ, 50+ per batch) -> 2 Screen (fresh freight, profit) -> 3 Draft (store template, shipping profile, weights, CJ inventory, alt text) -> 4 Gate (view photos, 5 checks, competitor) -> 5 Owner says publish -> 6 Review after 14 days (keep / fix / drop).

## Weekly rhythm
Mon store health (read only) · Tue CJ research batch · Wed draft + gate · Thu social posts/pins drafted · Fri ads/email review, customer messages · Sat rest · Sun owner reads report and decides.

## 30-day plan (from 2026-10-09)
- Week 1: Meta pixel + catalog connected and verified; policy text pasted into Settings > Policies; payment method checked; 4 provisional-pass drafts price-confirmed; decide 8 bad-photo live products; domain/email decision.
- Week 2: colour photos for 16 variants; fix or draft branded products; publish passing drafts; Merchant Center misrepresentation fix; sitemap/indexing check.
- Week 3: pins and posts for top 10 products; welcome + abandoned-cart emails; one paused test ad only if pixel is verified.
- Week 4: store health check and diagnosis (no visits / no add-to-cart / no checkout / no order); keep-fix-drop for week-2 products; next month plan.

## Ads
Pixel and purchase event confirmed first. Campaigns created paused; owner sets budget and starts. One campaign, 2-3 ad sets, 7+ days before judging. Suggested stop rule: pause an ad set after spend of about 2x the product's profit with no purchase.

## Credit habits
One task per message; plan before work; batch drafts; analysis-only first; reuse saved reports instead of repeating audits; photo review only on products that pass the money tests.
