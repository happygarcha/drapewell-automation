## Canadian privacy rights, tools we use, and marketing messages

**Where we operate.** Drapewell is an online store based in Surrey, British Columbia, Canada. We handle your personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where it applies, the privacy laws of the province or state where you live.

**Services we use.** To run the store we share the information needed for each job with these providers, who process it for us under their own privacy terms:
- **Shopify** for the store, checkout and payments.
- **Our fulfillment and shipping partners**, including CJ Dropshipping and its carriers, who receive your name, delivery address, phone number if you gave one, and order details so they can ship your order and provide tracking.
- **Klaviyo** for emails about your order and, if you agree, marketing emails.
- **Judge.me** if you choose to leave a product review.
- **Google, Meta and Pinterest** for website analytics and advertising measurement, which may use cookies or similar tools to see how people use the store and whether our ads work.

**Marketing messages.** We send marketing emails only with your consent, in line with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us to stop. Order, shipping and account messages are not marketing and may still be sent.

**Your choices.** You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent for marketing, by emailing happygarcha2018@hotmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. You can also use our [Your Privacy Choices](/pages/data-sharing-opt-out) page, and you can turn off cookies in your browser, although some parts of the store may not work without them.

**Where your information is stored.** Our providers may store or process information in Canada, the United States or other countries, where it may be accessed under local laws.

**Questions or complaints.** Email happygarcha2018@hotmail.com first. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).
