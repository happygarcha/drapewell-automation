## Method and caveats

- Landed CAD = (CJ product USD + freight USD) x 1.42443. Profit = 0.971 x (price + band) - 0.30 - landed. Bands: up to 300 g CA$9.99, up to 600 g CA$14.99, up to 900 g CA$19.99.
- Variant picked = cheapest in-stock variant listed with a variantSellPrice (CJ product cost uses variantSellPrice, not the search-result range). Colour variants within a product share price and weight unless noted.
- Freight line = cheapest non-Sensitive line delivering in 15 days or less. In every case that was CJPacket Ordinary (4-7 days). CJPacket JYSP Sensitive was cheaper by about US$0.40 but is a Sensitive line, so it was not used. Exceptions are noted per row.
- Suggested price is the lowest .99 price giving profit of at least CA$12. Several prices come out under CA$11.99, below the preferred CA$12 to CA$35 band. Raising them is a merchandising call.
- Competitor price not checked for any row. No demand, sales or competitor figures were gathered. listedNum is CJ's count of stores listing the item; a very low listedNum (0 to 5) means almost nobody sells it yet, which is not evidence of demand.
- Every PASS depends on customers accepting a CA$9.99 to CA$14.99 shipping charge on top of the item price, which makes checkout totals about 2x the item price on the cheapest products. That is a conversion risk that was not tested.
- Freight on CJPacket Ordinary scales almost linearly with weight (about US$3.9 + US$0.0103 per gram up to roughly 300 g) and did not react to box volume except on the two bulky rows flagged REJECT.
