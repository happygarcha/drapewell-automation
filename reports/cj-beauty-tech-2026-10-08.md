## Method notes

- Landed CAD = (CJ USD + freight USD) x 1.42443. Price = lowest .99 giving profit >= CA$12 where profit = 0.971 x (price + band) - 0.30 - landed. Checkout = price + band. Band: <=300 g 9.99, <=600 g 14.99.
- Freight line: CJPacket Ordinary, 4-7 days, chosen as the cheapest non-Sensitive line within 15 days. CJPacket JYSP Sensitive is a few cents cheaper but Sensitive-only and 7-15 days, so not used. Electronic/magnet items (magnetic ring holders, magnet cable winders) may need a Sensitive line; confirm before listing.
- Variant chosen = cheapest listed variant. CJ variant data has no per-variant stock (inventoryNum null); product-level warehouse inventory was >=100 in every search (startWarehouseInventory 100).
- Competitor figures: WebSearch is US-only. Restricted to walmart.ca / amazon.ca / canadiantire.ca / shoppersdrugmart.ca domains it returned CAD listings snapshots (dates unclear, some stale). Typical = middle of the cluster of generic items of the same type, not an exact match for each CJ item. Treat all as INDICATIVE. Temu and Etsy pages were not retrievable: "not found". Ratio = our checkout / typical.
- Verdict rule used: PASS needs ratio <= 1.5; MARGINAL 1.5-1.8; REJECT above 1.8 (priced out) or out of band; unchecked items with passing profit are CONDITIONAL.
