# CJ replacement product search, 2026-10-08

Read-only research. Nothing was created in Shopify or CJ. Photos not checked (CJ CDN blocked). Competitor prices not checked and none invented. No demand or sales figures exist for any item here.

## Method

- 26 CJ keyword searches (30 results each, about 780 listings screened) across bath, kitchen, living room decor, bedroom, pets, outdoors, desk and hobby, beauty tools, fashion accessories and gifts. Price filter 2.50-12 USD.
- 37 products opened with get_product_variants for weight and variant prices; 25 passed the weight limit and got a live calculate_freight quote to Canada (origin CN).
- Freight used is CJPacket Ordinary (4-7 days, the cheapest line that is not the slower 'JYSP Sensitive'; the two were within 0.4 USD). Freight quotes are for one unit, default variant noted in the table.
- Landed CAD = (CJ USD + freight USD) x 1.42443. Shipping band charged to customer: <=300g 9.99, <=600g 14.99. Profit = 0.971 x (price + band) - 0.30 - landed.
- All 25 clear CA$12 profit even at CA$25.00 (lowest was 13.75). Each extra CA$1 of price adds about CA$0.97 profit.
- Suggested price is the lowest .99 price giving at least CA$15 profit, with a floor of 25.99. It is a cost-based floor, not a market price. Competitor check (checkout total within about 1.5x typical) is still required before listing: marked 'not checked'.
- Handles in reports/gate-live-catalog-2026-10-08.csv were compared by theme; none of these duplicate an existing handle, but the two totes, three brush sets and two scarves overlap each other, so pick one of each.
