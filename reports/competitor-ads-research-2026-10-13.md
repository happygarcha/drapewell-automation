# Competitor ads and Pinterest keyword research, 2026-10-13

Read-only research. Sources: Pinterest MCP (get_related_keywords, get_suggested_keywords) and Meta Ad Library search (ACTIVE ads, countries CA + US). No ad text bodies, prices, spend or sales are shown by these tools, so none are reported. "Long-running" = start date more than 4 weeks before 2026-10-13.

## Headline result

- No ad in any search had been running more than 4 weeks. The oldest relevant ad found started 2026-10-10 (3 days). The oldest ad of any kind in the results started 2026-10-01 (unrelated short-drama ads). The search returns newest ads first and has no sort or start-date filter, so the library view here is biased to brand-new ads and cannot confirm long-running winners.
- Pinterest tools returned no volume or trend metric at all. Autocomplete (get_suggested_keywords) returned only the seed term itself for all 13 seeds. Related keywords returned only for 8 of 13 seeds.
- Meta results were loose text matches: many hits were unrelated (romance-novel apps, health ads). Only the relevant advertisers are listed below.

## Top signals (strongest first, all weak)

1. Crochet is the busiest niche: We Are Knitters runs 8 ads in the top 10 for "crochet kit", Mary Annette 5 and Crochetta yarn 5 for "crochet blanket", all started 2026-10-12/13. Evidence: ad IDs and start dates in the Ad Library results. Signal of testing, not of sales. Pinterest related keywords for crochet kit (crochet blanket, crochet patterns, how to crochet for beginners) matched.
2. Makeup brush holder: Prsefusoa.SE02 runs 5 ads with one identical title and Manifeist-p reuses it, started 2026-10-11/12. Pinterest "makeup brush holder" and "makeup organizer" match the seed.
3. Canvas wall art: at least 6 pages advertise it in the top 10 and 4 of them show 50% off style offers in the ad title (Artexplore, Mavren Art, CaliCuration holiday gifting). Discount-led competition.
4. Fall door swag: only two small advertisers (Silvio Peron Jr. started 2026-10-10, Vivian Johnson 2026-10-12) among 44 results, so a thin niche on Meta. Pinterest related shows "door decorations".
5. Pet carrier bag: Dexy Paws advertises a "Beige Pet Travel Bag" in CAD from 2026-10-13, a Canada-facing competitor, but 805 results are mostly non-carrier pet ads. Too new to judge.

None of these is a long-running ad. No ad in this research is a proven winner.

## Limits

- Pinterest: no volume, trend or competition metric is returned by either tool. Autocomplete only echoed the seed. Related keywords were empty for bath mat, shower curtain, canvas wall art, dog sweater, cushion cover (one retry also empty). The related list for the multi-term call mixed in unrelated terms (e.g. fall wedding, 3d tattoos). The tools take no region parameter, so CA vs US was not selectable.
- Meta Ad Library: results are ordered newest-first, no sorting or start-date filter, so older long-running ads were not reachable and the 4-week check could not be fulfilled. Search is a loose text match, so most hits were unrelated. Only the top 10-15 ads per query were read, out of estimated totals in the hundreds to thousands. Ad body text, product images, prices, spend, impressions and sales are not returned (only title, page name, dates, currency and a snapshot link). Prices and offers are reported only where they appear in the ad title. Snapshot images were not opened.
- Ad currency shows the advertiser billing currency, not necessarily the market (e.g. EUR, PLN, AUD, BRL ads appeared with CA/US filters).
- Weeks-running values are computed from start date to 2026-10-13; every relevant ad is under 1 week old.
- Ad libraries do not show sales or spend. "Many ads for one product" is a testing signal only.
- Seeds with no useful Meta data (bath mat, shower curtain, dog sweater, 3d wooden puzzle, cushion cover, christmas table runner, knitted throw blanket) should be re-searched with narrower phrases or specific competitor page IDs if the owner wants them analysed.
