# Competitor price and demand check A (2026-10-07)

READ-ONLY research. Nothing was published or changed.

## Method and limits (read first)
- WebSearch is US-only and returned almost no Amazon.ca, Temu/AliExpress CA or Etsy CA listings. Most price evidence is walmart.com (US, currency not labelled, assumed USD) or scattered Canadian retailers. Canada-shipped totals including shipping were NOT found for any product.
- "Indicative" figures = US listing price (+ shipping where shown) x 1.39 CAD/USD. Shipping and duty to Canada are not included unless stated. These are weak evidence; "typical" is "not found" everywhere because no listing set was large enough to define one.
- Ratio = our checkout / the HIGHEST indicative competitor figure found (the most favourable to us). PRICE-RISK if ratio > 1.5. Ratio against lower figures is worse.
- Ubersuggest: user_limits showed monthly metric updates 0/10 used, but the daily report quota (3) was exhausted after 3 keyword_overview calls (Canada, locId 2124). All other keywords: no data (HTTP 403 daily reports limit). Not retried. A paid plan raises the daily limit: https://app.neilpatel.com/en/pricing
- CPC currency was not stated by the tool; numbers reported as returned.
