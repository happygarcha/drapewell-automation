# Competitor price and keyword check B (products 17-32), 2026-10-07

Read-only research. Method and limits (important):
- Prices come from WebSearch snippets of Amazon.ca / Walmart.ca / Etsy listings (search restricted to those domains). Figures are LISTING prices in CAD as shown in the snippet. Shipping to Canada was NOT shown in almost any snippet, so "total incl. shipping" was NOT found; competitor totals below are item price only (a lower bound). Real ratios are therefore equal or lower than shown. USD converted at 1 USD = 1.39 CAD.
- Temu / AliExpress / Shopify stores: not searched successfully (no data returned); treated as "not found".
- Typical = median or representative listing price from the few listings found (small samples, not statistically reliable).
- Ubersuggest: user_limits showed 3 reports per day; keyword_overview returned HTTP 403 "daily reports limit: 3" on every call (Canada, locId 2124). Result: no data for every keyword. Not retried. keyword_metrics (10/month quota) was not used.
- PRICE-RISK = our checkout / typical competitor price > 1.5.
- Verdict rule used: KEEP needs comparable-or-higher competitor price AND visible demand evidence (no Ubersuggest data, so only review counts seen in listings); DROP when checkout clearly above market (ratio about 2 or more); otherwise MAYBE.
