## Limits

- Draft products are not served, so HTTP 200, per-page markup and the real JSON-LD could not be tested. Schema was judged by applying `theme/snippets/seo-meta.liquid` to the product data: no noindex for products, price in cart currency (CAD), availability from variants (inventory is not tracked, so every variant shows InStock), shippingDetails by weight band (<=300 g CA 9.99, <=600 g CA 14.99), MerchantReturnNotPermitted plus refund policy link, brand = vendor (Drapewell), at most 6 images, no aggregateRating or review. Live template tested in earlier runs (https://drapewell.myshopify.com/products/modern-bath-set), not re-run.
- Inventory: this API version has no `isActive` field on InventoryLevel; "active at cjdropshipping" is inferred from a level existing at gid://shopify/Location/115880919349 (each variant also has a level at 115880493365). CJ stock levels were not checked.
- Freight: quotes are the draft report's fresh CJPacket Ordinary quotes (CN to CA, M5V 2T6, qty 1, heaviest variant), not re-quoted by me; I re-did the profit arithmetic (0.971 x (price + band) - 0.30 - (CJ USD + freight USD) x 1.42443) and it matches for all 6 (14.27, 17.70, 14.24, 14.91, 14.10, 16.70).
- Competitor prices are WebSearch snippets limited to Amazon.ca, Walmart.ca and Best Buy.ca: single snapshots, shipping excluded, third-party sellers, mixed pack sizes (CAXUSD is a 3-pack), comparables not identical, medians over only the prices named. An unrestricted search returned US prices only and was not used. Product 3 has no like-for-like price. Nothing was invented; the owner confirms by hand before any publish.
- Photos were viewed at 800 px (crops enlarged for 2b, 2c, 5a, 5d, 5e, 6d). "No brand" means none readable; 6f box positions were estimated from the 800 px view. Dropped CJ photos could not be fetched (proxy blocks cf.cjdropshipping.com).
- Ubersuggest was unavailable (daily reports and keywords quota used), so no keyword demand for any of the six.
- Not done: Merchant Center or channel feed checks, GEO test in a real AI engine, sitemap or hreflang checks.
