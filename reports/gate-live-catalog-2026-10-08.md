## Rules applied
SEO: product title 45-70 chars; `<title>` at most 60; meta description 130-155, ending as a sentence (not cut), no run-together words; at least 6 tags; at least one admin alt that is not empty, not "view N", not over 125 chars; product_type set; not noindex; no duplicate title or meta; description overlap by 5-word shingles (flag at 40 percent or more, shipping text excluded). Result: no duplicates or near-duplicates found between products.
Technical: HTTP 200, one H1, every variant weight over 0, price format, 3 or more images, SKU on every variant, available, cj: tag.
Copy: risk words (health/skin/medical/best/guarantee/hypoallergenic/anti-/BPA/waterproof/durable/protects/mildew etc.) in body or title, brand/trademark list, supplier patterns ("Packing list", "Product information", "Package includes:", CJ named in text, "Please note"), duplicated lines, run-together words, size/material/what's-included present, shipping sentence present.
GEO: a lead paragraph (45 words or fewer), all three of size, material, what's included, a who/use line, a delivery-time line, no superlatives. Returns wording in the body is noted only.
