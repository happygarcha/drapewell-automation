## What would fix each FAIL

- C (competitor only): every other check passes. Options: (1) reprice. At 17.99 the checkout is 27.98 = 1.48x the median of 18.9 and profit stays about CA$14.5 (formula 0.971 x (17.99 + 9.99) - 0.30 - (3.00 + 5.67) x 1.42443); 18.99 gives checkout 28.98 (1.5x), profit about CA$15.5. (2) The owner confirms by hand that comparable teddy-collar twill vests with pockets sell near 28-31 (Queenmore 30.99 and the 26-28 coats are the nearest matches), and accepts 21.99. Either way it then passes with no other work. Price change is the owner's call.
- D photos: either the owner accepts the camellia brooch (no readable brand), or remove photos 3 and 4. Removing photo 4 also removes the Yellow variant image, so the Yellow variant would have to go or get a new clean photo, leaving 2 photos and 2 colours, which is under the 4-photo bar. A real fix needs 2 or more clean replacement photos (own photos, or crops that exclude the brooch, handbag and sunglasses). CJ's other photos were already rejected in the first run.
- D copy: drop "Soft" and "knit" (title, SEO description, first line, fabric bullet), e.g. title "Women's Tassel Scarf Wrap, 67 x 185 cm, Polyester, 3 Colours" (58 chars) and "polyester fabric, retro style" in the meta (keep 130-155).
