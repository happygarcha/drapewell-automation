## 4. What NOT to do

- No fake, seeded, or AI-written reviews, testimonials, or star ratings, and no review schema without real reviews.
- No invented statistics (customers served, orders shipped, "years in business"), awards, press mentions, or founding year.
- No claims of local manufacturing or in-house production. Orders are made and shipped by supplier partners.
- No claims of fast or guaranteed delivery beyond the stated estimates, and no "free shipping" or "duty-free" wording beyond what the policy says.
- No promise of easy returns. State plainly: no change-of-mind returns or exchanges.
- Do not leave llms.txt unmaintained or point it at dead URLs; review it quarterly and whenever collections or policies change.
- Do not state that llms.txt is a formal standard or guarantees citations. It is a community convention with unproven effect on AI answers.
- Keep policy wording identical across the About page, FAQ, llms.txt and policy pages. Contradictions reduce the chance of being cited.
