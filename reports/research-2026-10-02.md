Screened 63 new candidates across five rooms. Result: 8 pass, 22 marginal (test), 33 rejected. 6 draft listings created (none live).

## Held back

- Foam soap dispenser (battery): US battery route leaves about CA$4 profit on the 2-pack.
- Boho tapestry (bedroom) and mandala tapestry: overlap with the moon tapestry; list one first.

## Marginal (test later)

Bed runner, vortex rug, blackout curtains (about 3x Walmart.ca), under-bed storage bag, round placemats, silicone oven mitts (no heat rating), chenille hanging towel, dachshund doormat, snowman doormat, Christmas flannel throw, 24-piece ornament set, bubble glass vase, ceramic candle tray, iron candle holders, round elastic table cover, soap dispensers (two), mandala tapestry.

## Rejected (main reasons)

Too heavy (over 1.2 kg), supplier-shipped with no Canada/US route, shipping too costly for the price, price 1.5x or more above competitors, or too similar to existing products.
