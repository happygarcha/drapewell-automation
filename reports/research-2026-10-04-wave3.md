# Product research wave 3, 4 October 2026

Read-only screening of CJ Dropshipping by three agents (Christmas/winter, Bedroom/Living Room, Bath/Kitchen/Tech/Pet). Nothing was created or published. Each agent deep-checked only a few candidates (3, 5 and 14) because CJ search results are noisy and its freight responses are very large, so coverage is partial: about 22 deep-checked, 6 pass, 5 marginal.

Competitor prices are web-search snippets only (Amazon.ca, Walmart.ca and Etsy could not be opened). Freight is the cheapest CJ option (CJPacket Ordinary for Canada). Profit = 0.971 x (price + shipping charged) - 0.30 - (CJ cost + CJ freight, USD) x 1.42443, heaviest variant, Canada shipping.

## Marginal

- Thanksgiving pumpkin linen-look table runner (1706868083924672512): profit CA$16.17 at CA$16.99 plus 9.99 shipping, but 13-25 day delivery misses Canadian Thanksgiving (12 Oct); only works for US Thanksgiving (26 Nov) if live by about 1 Nov.
- Modern minimalist blackout curtains (1770792814083842048): profit CA$17.93, but total CA$44.98 is above 1.5x competitor singles; cutting price drops profit under CA$12. Blackout claim unproven.
- Bedroom full blackout curtains (1451820818521788416): profit CA$13.93 at CA$34.99 plus 24.99; poor variant sizes; no competitor price found.
- Thick lamb fleece pet blanket (1863873696726142976): profit CA$14.63 but total CA$39.98 is about 2x comparable pet blankets.
- Adjustable aluminium laptop stand (1435161793629655040): profit CA$13.97 but total CA$49.98 is far above comparable stands; crowded product.

## Rejected (main reasons)

Near-duplicates of existing items, tiny low-value single pieces (bandanas, ornaments, single cushion covers), wireless or battery items, and cheap commodity items where the 1.5x competitor cap blocks the CA$12 profit floor.

## Not covered

Bath towels and bath textiles, pet mats, kitchen textiles, rugs, tapestries, canvas prints, lamp shades, baskets, pillowcases and throw blankets in Bedroom/Living Room. Re-run these lanes in smaller batches.
