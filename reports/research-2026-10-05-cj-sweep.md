## Lessons
- CJ freight to CA is about US$4.6-12 by weight (70 g 4.63, 170 g 5.67, 320 g 7.41, 500 g 9.6, 700 g 11.9). Products whose market price is below ~CA$25 cannot work.
- Bulky items (wreaths in big boxes, racks) lose the cheap Ordinary line.
- Sweet spot: light products (under 600 g) that sell for CA$35+ elsewhere (desk mats, tapestries, throws, table runners).
- All stock is China factory stock; no CJ/CA/US warehouse stock found.
- Not searched: curtains, unframed prints, Thanksgiving items, baskets (see lane notes).
