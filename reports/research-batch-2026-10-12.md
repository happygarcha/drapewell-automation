# CJ research batch, 2026-10-12 (read-only; nothing created, nothing published)

Goal from owner: find about 10 products worth approving. Result: 4 candidates plus 1 risk after screening; the rest are unscreened or rejected. Fewer than 10, see "Why not 10".

## Method and limits
- 9 CJ catalogue searches (placemats, dog bowls/feeding mats, cushion covers, canvas totes, camping lanterns, cat tunnels, dog winter sweaters, women's winter slippers, winter socks sets, kitchen racks, wall clocks), about 250 listings read. Cost filter 1.5 to 10 USD, stock 200+.
- Money test per docs/dropshipping-playbook.md: profit = 0.971 x (price + shipping band) - 0.30 - landed; landed = (CJ USD + freight USD) x 1.42443; need >= CA$12.
- Freight: one real CJ quote (Toronto M5V 2T6, CJPacket Ordinary): lick mat 255 g = 6.65 USD. Formula freight = 3.78 + 0.0113 x grams matches that quote (6.66) and the earlier batch quotes (135 g 5.31, 220 g 6.27, 420 g 8.50, 620 g 10.99), so the other rows use it. They are ESTIMATES until each finalist gets its own fresh quote at the draft stage.
- Competitor price: WebSearch is US-biased; CAD evidence found only for the bowl riser (homesalive.ca) and dish racks (Canadian Tire, Walmart.ca). Everything else "not checked". No competitor figure here is invented.
- Not done: photo review, Ubersuggest demand (daily quota not used), electronics skipped (battery/sensitive shipping lines).

## Why not 10
- Cheap commodity items (placemats, totes, lick mats, cushion covers) fail the competitor-price test: our CA$ checkout is about 2x shelf prices. Heavy items (bowls, clocks, racks) fail the 600 g target or push shipping to 15-25.
- Only light products with CA$22+ perceived value pass: pet clothing, seasonal goods, shoes.
- CJ detail pages are huge (5-10k tokens each), so screening more than about 10 products per session is expensive.
