# Beauty research, 2026-10-18 (read-only)

Request: find at least 10 new beauty products that pass. Nothing was created, published or changed in Shopify or CJ.

**Result: 1 provisional PASS (one product family), not 10.** The bar was not lowered. Reasons are below.

## Method
- Store check: 5 beauty-related products are already ACTIVE (32-piece makeup brush set, 4-piece satin eye mask/pillowcase/hair-tie set, three rotating or press-pop brush holders). Not duplicated.
- Earlier beauty sweep (reports/research-beauty-tech-2026-10-13.md): about 330 CJ listings read, only the brush-holder family passed, and it is already live.
- This pass: 8 more CJ searches (brush sets, makeup bags, bonnets, claw clips, drawers/organisers, heatless curlers, pillowcases, hair towels), 6 product details, 1 real freight quote.
- Money formulas from docs/dropshipping-playbook.md: landed CAD = (CJ USD + freight USD) x 1.42443; band 9.99 up to 300 g, 14.99 up to 600 g; profit = 0.971 x (price + band) - 0.30 - landed; need >= CA$12; checkout within about 1.5x typical Canadian competitor total.
- Freight: one real quote (CJPacket Ordinary, Toronto M5V 2T6, 250 g makeup brush set: USD 6.59), which equals the playbook formula 3.78 + 0.0113 x grams. Others use the formula (E).
- Competitor prices: a helper ran WebSearch limited to Canadian retailers. Everything is search-snippet evidence, mostly amazon.ca, NOT hand-checked. Photos were not viewed. No demand or sales numbers are used or claimed. Ubersuggest not used.

## What would unlock 10
Under the current rules the beauty accessory pool is nearly exhausted; changing the search terms will not give 10 more. The owner would need to choose one of:
1. Widen the category to adjacent personal-care or vanity-and-bath items with Canadian shelf prices of about CA$22 or more.
2. Accept a looser checkout ratio for specific items (an owner decision; not applied here).
3. Accept bundles (for example a brush set with a makeup bag) as a way to lift the shelf price. CJ sells these only as separate SKUs, so this needs a manual bundle listing.
