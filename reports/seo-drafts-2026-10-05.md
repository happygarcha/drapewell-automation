Notes:
- The brief says 19 products but lists 20. All 20 are covered.
- Character counts were done by hand. Check them in Shopify's search listing preview before saving.
- Page titles keep the brand suffix ` | Drapewell` (11 characters), so the lead part is 49 characters or fewer.
- Every meta description ends with "Ships to Canada and the US." (27 characters). It makes no speed or free-shipping promise, because the products are dropshipped.
- Tags exclude the existing `cj:<pid>` tag. Where a CJ tag is present, keep it as is.
- Alt-text: write the first image as a descriptive scene. Write each colour variant image as `{Colour} {short product name}, {what the image shows}`. Never keyword-stuff.

- **Two winter 3-piece sets (#8 vs #9).** The head term "hat, scarf and gloves set" is shared. Assign ownership as follows.
  - #8 owns "women's knitted hat scarf gloves set" (Women's + Knitted).
  - #9 owns "fleece-lined hat scarf gloves set" (Fleece-Lined).
  - Never use "fleece-lined" in #8, or "women's" or "knitted" in #9's title.

### Product schema reminders (check the live page after publishing)
- **Offer:** `price` and `priceCurrency` = CAD must match the variant shown on the page. Variant ranges (#16 CA$19.99 to 29.99, #18 CA$29.99 to 34.99) need either per-variant offers or an AggregateOffer with `lowPrice`/`highPrice`. Check what the theme outputs.
- **Availability:** `InStock` or `OutOfStock` (do not leave `PreOrder` or `BackOrder` unless intended). #7 is seasonal: keep `priceValidUntil` or an expiry plan around 28 Nov 2026, and update the page afterwards.
- **Shipping:** add `shippingDetails` (OfferShippingDetails) for CA and US with handling and transit times that match the real store policy. Add `hasMerchantReturnPolicy`. The Shopify Google & YouTube channel and Merchant Center settings must match the schema.
- **Identifiers and media:** `brand` = Drapewell, `sku` per variant, a `gtin` or `mpn` if you have one, otherwise omit it (do not invent). Every variant image should be reachable and match the colour.
- **No fabricated reviews.** Do not add `aggregateRating` or `review` without real reviews. FAQ schema is optional and not needed.
- **Drafts to active.** Confirm the products are not `noindex`, are in the sitemap, and that titles and meta are saved in the SEO fields (not left at defaults). Request indexing only after the copy is final.
- **Claims alignment.** Any waterproof, fleece-lined, non-slip or washable wording must also match Merchant Center product attributes, or products can be disapproved.
